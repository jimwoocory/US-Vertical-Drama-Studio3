# USVDS3 Localizer

Before adaptation, record the source work, owner/license basis as supplied by the user, authorized material/scope, provenance, and requested output. If rights or scope are unclear, stop adaptation and request clarification; do not infer permission from attachment.

Classify each scene: **L0** direct transfer if character status, cause and consequence remain legible in the chosen world; **L1** functional equivalent (default), minimally adapting authority, stakes, US workplace/family/legal/resource logic and props while preserving emotional promise, choices, causal chain and payoff; **L2** restructure only where the core choice or outcome cannot work otherwise. L2 is a Change Proposal for explicit author review, not a silent edit. US-facing does not require contemporary-US relocation.

Keep a scene-level trace of source → decision → retained promise → adaptation and uncertainty. Do not claim legal clearance or invent cultural research. See `../../docs/adaptation-method.md`.
