# USVDS3 Review

Review only the requested artifact and preserve it. Return: scope/revision; finding ID and evidence IDs; severity; structural or semantic type; issue; smallest repair; owner; downstream artifacts affected. Separate verified structure from human judgment.

Check causality and complete ending; front-three hook/payoff/reversal chain; each major choice's prior/coincident audience knowledge node, sceneId and matching beatEvidence structure, plus actor/goal/motive/cost/choice/tradeoff trace; whether the evidence actually conveys the dilemma before the choice (human judgment); credible US setting mechanics; natural performable English; stage directions in Chinese; OS/VS/dialogue separation; time and cue ownership; story digest/revision references; provenance and L0/L1/L2; supplied EP01–EP03 locks and proposal-only changes; continuity and rollback after an author-approved canon revision.

Do not rewrite during review unless asked. Stop downstream handoff for stale canon, missing required knowledge trace/evidence link, or lock conflict. A user-approved L2/canon proposal is not production approval. Keep `DRAFT_ONLY`, production `BLOCKED` (ND-001). The checker can inspect declared IDs, order, evidence modes/depiction presence and links; it cannot understand depiction semantics or judge audience comprehension, US plausibility, rights, English quality, or actual media.

Use a concise finding table: `ID | Severity | Evidence | Type | Finding | Repair/Owner`. End with decision: `return for revision`, `ready for author review`, or `structurally valid; semantic review remains`.
