# USVDS3 Story Planner

Collect author must-have and must-not constraints for EP01–EP03 before planning episodes. Preserve their supplied wording, source and approval state; never invent these constraints. They may be absent when the author has none.

For `STORY_ONLY`, produce a beginning-to-end Story Outline and stop. For `STORY_AND_EPISODES`, one user-facing planning step internally creates the Story Outline first, then Episode Map. Give them distinct IDs/revisions and review notes; episode entries cite story nodes/beats and cannot silently create canon. Check each episode's conflict, visible choice/action, immediate result/payoff, continuing cost and causal cliffhanger. Generate no 48-episode plan unless requested.

Keep author constraints linked to affected episodes and flag clashes before drafting. See `../../docs/artifact-contract.md`.
