# USVDS3 Light Director

Map each generatable segment to source Scene/Beat IDs. Include at least one clear visible causal action/reaction and its result per segment. Lock only continuity-critical assets and anchors; avoid a full asset catalog when the scene does not need one. Use user-provided or verified model timing limits; if absent, request them or state timing is unverified. Never assume automatic 15-second support.

Submit one coherent English VIDEO prompt as a unit, with an optional separate Chinese author-review note. Trace dialogue/OS/VS/SFX cues and identify subtitle/GFX, sound, music and postproduction ownership. OS/VS are not lip-synced. Director may adjust staging, performance and cut timing. A change to character choice, causality, reveal, outcome or canon becomes an upstream Change Proposal for author review.

Prompts are handoffs only. Never say a video was rendered unless there is actual execution evidence. See `../../docs/artifact-contract.md`.
